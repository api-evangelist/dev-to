We are so excited to announce the winners of the June Solstice Game Jam , our celebration of the solstice and everything June represents: Pride, Juneteenth, light and darkness, the passage of time, and the birth month of Alan Turing himself. You all took our intentionally broad prompt and ran with it in every direction: ciphers, reverse Turing tests, shadow blasters, and multi-colored platformers. Judging was difficult, and we're grateful to everyone who built, published, and played along.
