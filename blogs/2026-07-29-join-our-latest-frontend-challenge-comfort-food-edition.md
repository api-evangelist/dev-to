We're back with another Frontend Challenge, and this time we're hungry! 🍜🥧 Running through August 16 , Frontend Challenge: Comfort Food Edition invites you to build something inspired by the food that makes you feel at home. Show off the dish you make when nothing else will do, build a site for a restaurant that exists (or one that only lives in your head), share the recipe you've been perfecting for years, or put a spotlight on a regional dish that deserves more attention.
