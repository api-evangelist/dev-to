We are excited to announce the winners of our DEV Weekend Challenge: Generosity Edition ! We asked you to build something in the spirit of generosity and left the interpretation wide open. Over one weekend you built grant radars that refuse to trust their own model , a hospital night shift where your starting money was left by the last player , and matchmakers that turn three boxes of storybooks into an actual plan .
